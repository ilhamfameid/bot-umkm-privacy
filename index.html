Privacy Policy - Bot UMKM Demo

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Bot UMKM Demo ("the Service") is an automated ordering assistant that lets customers of a small business place orders through messaging apps such as WhatsApp and Telegram. The Service is operated by Ilham Fameidana ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Service handles and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Messages you send to the bot, such as menu choices, quantities, and delivery address.
  • Messaging identifiers, such as your WhatsApp phone number or Telegram user ID, which are needed to reply to you.
  • Location, only if you choose to share it with the bot for delivery purposes.
  • Order details: items ordered, total price, time, and delivery address.

We do not ask for passwords, payment card numbers, or government ID numbers.

2. How we use information

  • To process and record your order and reply to your messages.
  • To notify the business owner about new orders so they can prepare and deliver them.
  • To fix errors and keep the Service working.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Where information is stored and who can see it

  • Order records are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by the business using the Service, and in a database used by the bot to keep conversations and backups.
  • Messages are delivered through the messaging platforms you use (for example WhatsApp, operated by Meta, or Telegram), which process them under their own privacy policies.
  • Information is accessible only to the business owner and the operator of the Service, and is shared with other parties only when required by law.

4. Retention

Order records are kept for as long as the business needs them for its operations, and conversation state is kept only briefly (typically a few hours). We keep information no longer than necessary.

5. Your choices and data deletion

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information associated with your account (for example, your phone number and order history) by emailing ilhamfamei1@gmail.com with the phone number or user ID you used. We will respond within a reasonable time and delete the data we hold unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including restricted access to the spreadsheet and server credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the date above.

8. Contact

Ilham Fameidana - ilhamfamei1@gmail.com
